Scope

The user's Aussie–Thai parma and steak-wrap images were used as the quality
benchmark for ingredient purpose, cooking order, texture and coherent assembly.
The release improves shared regional cooking rules across meals and classics.
It does not publish the two supplied recipe images or establish kitchen testing.
See CULINARY_QUALITY_2026-10-27.txt for changes and culinary references.

Regional ingredients now have specific cooking stages. Paste frying and spice
blooming precede liquid; wet condiments avoid a dry hot pan; delicate ingredients
finish late. Woody infusions are removed, while spoon-measured star anise is
explicitly ground and bloomed. Dried ancho preparation precedes browning, and
long-smoke sauces are scheduled near service. Oil and other ingredient portions
are allocated once and scaled consistently.

Suitable Chinese-family boneless stir-fry meats receive a measured refrigerated
starch coating. Crisp mains keep sauce alongside. Classic formats retain their
original starch, seeded preparation happens before delicate finishing, pasta
does not wait drained during extra preparation, and commercial cold-smoked mains
stay chilled while their separate sauce is cooked and cooled.
