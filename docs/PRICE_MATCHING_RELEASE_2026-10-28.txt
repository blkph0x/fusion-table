The website and Android pricing safeguards are released. Reliable unattended
Coles prices are still not established, and the broader Coles goal is unfinished.

What changed

Shared checks now reject incompatible food identities, cuts, skin/bone states,
prepared meals, meat recipe-base packets, pet food and serving tools. Those
checks also protect retained browser prices and explicit product review; a
previously saved or reviewed candidate cannot bypass a hard food mismatch.

All thirty authored main identities were checked against twelve existing strong
preparation/style markers. Unrequested smoked, marinated, pickled or named-style
products are excluded. Explicitly requested matching styles, legitimate raw cuts,
Pekin duck breed wording, ordinary canned/cooked legumes and premium brand
review remain supported. The recipe-generator source was not changed by this
pricing release.

Shopping choices remain cheapest per item, one supermarket, and current-area
butcher/fruit-shop destinations. A chosen local shop without a usable quote keeps
its assignment and an unknown price. That item is omitted from the known subtotal;
another seller's price or a planning allowance is not substituted for that shop.

Verification and its limits

- The final website release passed 930 Linux tests, with no failures or skips,
  plus TypeScript and fourteen host account/SQL checks.
- Fifty staged and fifty public recipe pages/PDFs passed. The public fifty-page
  run did not include price checks.
- The deployed browser fixture rejected seven unsafe injected/cached candidates
  and preserved valid product/brand-review controls and the mobile layout.
  All API/image responses were intercepted: these are interface tests, not live
  supplier, account or image-generation coverage. Positive price controls were
  synthetic and are not published shop quotes.
- Five fresh staged recipe-price cases returned thirty sampled ingredient rows.
  Independent review found no selected/eligible food or quantity errors among
  200 offers, including 34 eligible offers and eight selected online guides.
  The overall run remains FAILED because it returned no eligible Coles offers.
- The earlier full fifty-batch capture exposed real mismatches. Corrected-source
  offline replays reject them and retain valid controls. They preserve the old
  prices/timestamps and do not establish new prices or fresh supplier coverage.

A full fresh public fifty-recipe price run on this final safety release remains
unpassed. No claim is made that every ingredient has an observed shop price,
that all shop stock is confirmed, or that structural checks establish taste.
