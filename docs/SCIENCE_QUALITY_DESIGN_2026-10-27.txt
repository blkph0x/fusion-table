Solver correctness
SAT assignments are checked against their original clauses. UNSAT is returned only
after independent RUP replay of the learned-clause certificate reaches contradiction.
Resource caps, incomplete decisions and proof failures are UNKNOWN, not UNSAT.
The model enumerator blocks every full assignment previously emitted. Exhaustion is
complete only when the remaining formula is independently certified UNSAT.
The generic optimizer exposes optimal only for complete finite enumeration; bounded
runs expose best-known. Exhaustive small truth tables and independent random-formula
oracles test model sets, optimum values, source binding and certificate rejection.

Website selection
An existing plate keeps its main, method and side. Complete-plate hard clauses keep
regional boundaries, ingredient exclusions and at most two compatible additions.
The interactive path examines up to eight checked finishes, scoring ingredient
simplicity, produce, pantry reuse and extra-pan effort. This is a transparent soft
objective over the examined finishes, not a global taste ranking. A resource-limited
search preserves the already validated authored base instead of relaxing filters.
Independent recipe postconditions reject malformed portions/timing, duplicate shopping
identities, unsuitable raw kidney-bean/cassava forms, fractional counted birds,
slow-braised liver and overfilled open shells. Separate-sauce cooking remains valid.
Large dry-starch/oil allocations are review warnings, not nutrition estimates.

Coverage and replay
scripts/audit-complete-catalogue.mjs dynamically enumerates the canonical authored
catalogue: every compatible dietary default or explicit cut/main/method row, every
region and every side, plus every classic/region/diet. At this checkpoint the target
is 20,414,810 inputs. They use medium heat, four servings and no random seed.
Five heat levels and twelve serving counts project to 1,224,888,600 inputs before
seeded regional-pantry sampling, cleaner options and arbitrary household text.
Those additional spaces are not described as exhaustively enumerated.
Each canonical case has a compressed witness containing its exact input, ingredient
identity/quantity references, timings, method/endpoint summary and issues. Worker
ingredient dictionaries preserve names, units, notes, aliases and allergen labels.
Chunks and dictionaries are hashed; resumable checkpoints verify committed records
and source/checker fingerprints. Partial work cannot become a completion certificate.
A separate finish-graph audit enumerates all feasible SAT finishes only for its named
representative contexts. Regional pantry seed outcomes are a different mechanism.

Cooked feedback and improvement
First impressions are separate from cooked reports. Low scores (0-2), No and will-not-
make-again require a structured reason, saved atomically with the vote. Other requires
useful written detail. Thirteen categories include salt, heat, texture, timing,
quantities, unclear steps, poor combinations, availability, cost and safety concerns.
An optional ingredient and method step must exist in the exact supplied recipe.
Server-side cooking identity includes actual ingredients, quantities, servings and
method content. Equal actual food can share feedback despite seed provenance; changed
food cannot inherit evidence from an old version or another region/diet.
Anonymous browser counts are not verified people. Repeated reports from one browser
cannot supply three independent browser opinions. Current cooked feedback has zero
ranking influence before three distinct browsers; its bounded adjustment only ranks
already-filtered candidates within existing variety/cleanliness bands, in a window of
sixteen. Preview and legacy reports never become cooked-ranking evidence.
Historical cooking/allergen concerns remain review requests and prevent positive
promotion. Reports do not automatically alter ingredients, cooking endpoints or hard
filters. Trends exposes reasons for editorial investigation. Personal cookbook stars
remain private and are labelled separately from shared reports.

Evidence limits
A passing rule audit establishes the checked, encoded properties of its versioned
catalogue. It does not prove taste, authenticity, nutrition, packet contents, allergy
safety or every free-text setting. Kitchen trials and reviewed cooked reports are
still needed to improve the authored culinary rules. New catalogue/rule versions
must rerun the audit; historical results cannot certify changed source.

Automatic recipe shopping estimates
Recipe and shared-recipe pages check available online guides and area-recorded
prices without a button press. The shopping workspace does the same after an area
or list change. Requests are small batches of six, at most two together; equivalent
views share in-flight work and a five-minute visit cache. Exact quantities, units,
area, country and nearby-shop context bind the cache. Reviewed selections and explicit
exclusions survive late automatic responses. Changed servings/pantry/area invalidate
old values. Pack/rate shopping costs are distinct from food consumed per serving.
Missing prices remain unknown and partial subtotals show the priced-item coverage.
Narrow plain-product rules can resolve common brand ties while excluding changed food
forms, unsuitable substitutes and non-food products. Australian tablespoons/teaspoons
convert to20/5ml only for volume packs; no food density or dry/cooked yield is guessed.
Loose-product weight increments without a verified selling-price basis are excluded.
Retailer outages and ambiguous products remain visible for manual review.

Persistent background pictures
The image endpoint responds with a cached picture or labelled waiting placeholder.
RecipeImage polls boundedly and swaps in the finished exact picture automatically.
A SHA256 key binds complete actual ingredients/quantities/method content, rather than
a truncated list or seed alone. Durable queue/cache files are atomic under
FUSION_DATA_DIR/plate-images. One leased renderer, bounded pending work, retries,
hourly attempt limits and disk limits prevent unbounded generation. Requested pictures
outrank warm jobs. Opt-in warming queues eight curated plates daily; an explicit
warm script can prime common exact tokens. It does not schedule20million renders.
Only the existing loopback local diffusion model is used; no cloud API/key was added.
The model requires host service supervision and its ready endpoint. Pictures are
illustrations of recipe ideas and do not establish actual cooking results.
