FUSION TABLE — WIDE CATALOGUE CULINARY QUALITY
27 October 2026

The user's Aussie–Thai parma and steak-wrap quality benchmark now extends beyond
the initial sauce rules to the complete meal catalogue, selected cuts and sides.
This pass retains the available region/main/method/side choices, measured portions
and hard dietary exclusions. It does not establish that every combination tastes
good or that these authored templates have been kitchen-tested.

Ingredient forms and regional methods

All 135 pantry defaults were reviewed with 18,240 regional cooking plans spanning
76 regions, gluten-free variants, five heat levels, authored/seeded profiles,
serving scales and oil/no-oil paths. Powdered mustard does not receive a whole-
seed popping instruction. Powdered or finely processed galangal, makrut leaf and
dried lime are not mistaken for removable woody pieces. Explicit dried herbs are
handled as dried ingredients, rather than being washed/chopped as fresh herbs.
Raw sesame is toasted and reserved during preparation, before delicate finishing;
ready-toasted sesame retains its existing handling. Allocation references ensure
a reserved or removed portion is not counted as a second use.

Every side and its dietary variants

All 90 side definitions were inspected. Side plans now identify substantial
cooking work and short final dressing/steaming/bread actions separately. The meal
method groups substantive side work together and schedules it alongside the main.
A second 30-minute vegetable roast no longer appears as work to start only after
a quick main is already cooked. Main steps tell the cook to read the finishing
window before starting; quick mains wait until longer sides are nearly ready.
Raw browning is not followed by an instruction to wait for a side.

Shared preparation specifies produce cuts and prepared-product conditions, with
equipment. Passive advance preparation is separate from the active cooking
window. Sticky rice includes soaking, draining and a steamer setup; its advance
soak estimate is visible in the full recipe and time total. Packet directions
still control the particular rice product. Cooked rice for fried rice has its
own ingredient identity, so a regional dry-rice rename cannot change its weight
basis. Leftover rice handling remains explicit.
Regional grain renaming is restricted to the measured rice portion in its actual
cooking step. It cannot rename rice miso or replace measured glaze water with a
rice-packet allowance. Coconut milk counts toward total cooking liquid when a
generic rice is regionalised, rather than receiving a second full water amount.

Miso aubergine uses a late glaze, soy tagging and an explicitly gluten-free miso
where required. Pandan rice, lemongrass rice and the banana-leaf wrapper include
their named ingredients and appropriate removal. Tostones soften, flatten and
fry again. Biryani-style rice, jewelled rice, harissa carrots, bravas sauce and
elote-inspired corn have measured flavour components rather than empty names.
The limited side seasoning is kept separate from the selected main's regional
sauce. Split oil portions are explicit and reconcile to the ingredient list.

Cassava is a special case. This short side now requires a commercially processed,
peeled, fully cooked product sold ready to reheat, with packet directions. It
does not promise that ordinary boiling for a stated number of minutes will make
raw/bitter/unidentified cassava safe. The restriction is visible in ingredients,
steps and technique notes, with a source reference in template notes.

Cuts, portions and primary cooking

Chosen mince and lean boneless cuts no longer inherit a slow joint's long simmer.
Slow oven cuts receive a covered moist stage and a tenderness check rather than
a short uncovered steak roast. Estimates still depend on actual piece weight,
thickness, equipment and the endpoint; a clock is not a tenderness guarantee.

Whole birds are listed as whole shopping units, rounded up for the requested
household, with a declared size assumption and surplus handling. The method uses
the actual packet weight, checks both breast and inner thigh to 75°C and includes
resting. Whole-bird cooking is not shortened because fewer people are eating.
Whole roast birds have their own oven instructions; wet methods use a suitably
deep pot. Poultry smoking does not inherit brisket bark/stall/wrap instructions.
Sausages are cooked as links before slicing into a stir-fry. Quick gizzard dishes
include a tenderising simmer beforehand, with its preparation time/equipment.
Tongue is tenderised in water and peeled while warm before its short finishing
method. Other offal gets appropriate trimming and even slices/pieces. Stock-only
chicken frames remain available to shopping/product matching but are not offered
as a meaty main-meal cut. The 30 mains, 266 default main/method combinations and
90 side choices are unchanged by this feasibility correction.

Simpler-method preferences no longer merge a cooking or delicate finishing step
into generic preparation merely because its prose mentions washing or prepared
ingredients. Explicit finishing markers survive. Concurrent main/sauce pans are
not silently rewritten into a shared pan. Classics requiring an animal-main
thermometer endpoint now include the thermometer in equipment.

Verification

The reusable opt-in script is scripts/audit-culinary-matrix.mjs. Its default is a
coverage plan; --full runs the complete authored balanced region/main-method/side
product, then seed/heat/serving/clean variants, supported cut/method variants,
dietary variants and classics. It records actual counts and source hashes before
and after the run. A source change invalidates the completed audit.

The final counts, runtime checks and deployment identity are recorded separately
in WIDE_CULINARY_DEPLOYMENT_2026-10-27.txt and evidence under
docs/audit-evidence-2026-10-27/wide-*. The full authored balanced product is
1,819,440 cases. Other dimensions are the explicitly recorded rotating cases,
not a claim to cover every random seed and every possible household setting.

Publication limits

Automated coverage checks structure, selection fidelity, ingredient references,
quantities, timings, declared allergens, method boundaries and endpoints. It
cannot assess taste, tenderness, cultural authenticity, a real product label or
cross-contact. Recipes remain template-generated and not kitchen-tested. The
native Android APK is a separate build; its corresponding signed release is
recorded in ANDROID_CULINARY_DEPLOYMENT_2026-10-27.txt.
