FUSION TABLE: COMPLETE INGREDIENT PLANNING VALUES AND PRICE GATHERING
27 October 2026

Coverage and honest price labels

Every authored ingredient/unit pair has an immediate Australian-dollar planning
value. These are authored budgeting allowances, not measured local shelf prices.
Food used in a recipe and the packs to buy have separate totals. Unknown product
prices remain unknown in the observed-price fields: a fallback allowance is never
inserted as if a retailer published it. The API includes planningEstimate beside
its existing observed offer fields, and the interface can show a value before a
network check completes.

Profiles distinguish whole birds, different meat cuts/breeds, seafood, raw and
ready-cooked starch, drained canned foods, fresh produce, herbs, specialist pastes,
spices, bread pieces, eggs, dairy and plant proteins. Metric spoon volumes are
exact: an Australian tablespoon is 20 ml and a teaspoon 5 ml. Dry-spice density, fresh
citrus yield, drained-can yield and estimated bread/shell counts are explicitly
documented assumptions only. They never become hidden conversions for observed
retailer offers. Unsupported free-text units are declined rather than guessed.

Observed product cost

Compatible current or reviewed offers replace allowances for the quantities they
can actually support. The consumed-food cost divides by the selling quantity;
the shopping cost rounds up purchase packs. Whole ingredients at home still have
a food-used cost even when the shopping shortfall is smaller. Exact name/unit
and quantity checks prevent reusing a different ingredient or pantry shortfall
as the full recipe cost.

Gathering fixes and diagnosis

Provider caches now retain 512 query results per provider/fetcher for ten minutes,
use least-recently-used eviction and share duplicate requests across meals. Each
caller cancels independently; the underlying work is cancelled when no views
still await it. A 30 second per-query negative cache and 60 second blocked-provider
backoff prevent repeated failed requests. Existing bounded provider concurrency
and queues remain. Abandoned results cannot populate the cache, and injected
test/provider fetch functions cannot contaminate one another's cache.

Woolworths responses are streamed and cancelled above 2 MB, including a declared
Content-Length check, instead of reading an unbounded body first. Coles retains
its bounded HTML parsing and transparent fallback when no readable public
product data is supplied. A third-party challenge is not bypassed.

Live diagnosis found and fixed a real wrong-food match: Brown Onion Instant Gravy
was previously eligible as a fresh onion price. Prepared-food exclusions now
reject gravy, bouillon, dips, chutneys, relishes and marmalade. Reversed wording
such as Onion Brown is accepted for the same plain produce. Narrow common-food
rules resolve safe brand ties for raw chicken breast, carrots, plain flour and
ordinary soy sauce while excluding cut/preparation/dietary alternatives.
An exact-volume bug that missed 1 L bottles has also been corrected. Authoring
suffixes such as 'for the side' no longer pollute product searches or matching;
food-state, drained/cooked and dietary requirements remain.

Evidence

96 focused tests pass across ingredient allowances, existing provider/matcher
tests, automatic offers, shopping-cost arithmetic and provider-cache races.
The canonical fixture includes all 568 distinct name/unit pairs from the six
completed authored-catalogue dictionaries (the 1,137 exact descriptors also
include notes/alias variants sharing these pricing inputs). Quantity tests run
each pair at 0.1,1,12 and 1000 units.

The final browser review also exposed old seeded finishes tagged as specialty
rather than produce. Exact fresh-food names now receive the appropriate fresh
profile regardless of that legacy category, while powders, pickles and sauces
cannot inherit it. Cucumber 100 g uses a 60-cent food allowance and snow peas
75 g a 131-cent allowance, with their separate pack assumptions displayed.
These are budgeting assumptions, not newly observed shelf prices. Version v2
adds this classification correction without changing recipe ingredients or methods.

The opt-in deterministic pricing audit passed 8,810 actual generated meals,
covering 149,954 ingredient lines, all 76 regions,90 sides,30 mains,13 methods and 5 diets.
It includes all 4,250 classic/diet/region combinations with rotating heat, serving
count and seed, plus 4,560 stratified ingredient-led meals with cut variants.
No ingredient line lacked an allowance and no invalid total was produced.
This proves the tested rules and coverage, not exact checkout totals or taste.

The small live provider diagnosis checks eight representative foods and records
every source, product, pack, offer and fallback. After the fixes, all eight had
compatible observed online quantity prices from Woolworths in about 1.1 seconds
from this execution host. Coles supplied no readable public product data and
its backoff/fallback worked. These are one-time unlocalized online observations,
not a permanent availability or local-price guarantee.
