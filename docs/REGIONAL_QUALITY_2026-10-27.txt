FUSION TABLE — REGIONAL QUALITY FOLLOW-UP
27 October 2026

The supplied audit identified real usability and composition problems. This pass
separates a pending builder choice from a completed recipe and constrains the
seeded finish on an already complete dish.

Verified causes and changes

1. A region button changed the builder's private state while the previous recipe
   remained visible. Navigating away remounted that previous recipe and lost the
   pending choice. Pending main/method/region/side/cut choices now survive view
   changes; the old ingredients are hidden behind a Create prompt until a new
   recipe is made. Classic controls also synchronize when reopening/restoring a
   recipe. Household preferences and saved recipe snapshots remain intact.

2. Independent seeded lanes could add tofu/fish/prawns to another complete main
   or turn a filled/crumbed dish into a cold stir-fry or wrap. Applying a finish
   now preserves the selected main, method and side, adds at most two new
   finishing ingredients, excludes a second protein and unnecessary extra sauce
   lanes, and gives every actual addition a preparation instruction. Broth
   finishes require measured retained cooking liquid and serve the chosen side
   alongside. Titles name actual additions instead of an as-plated ingredient
   string. The flavour explanation also names the actual sauce rather than
   unselected default seasonings. Explicit standalone solver admission tests
   retain their API behavior.

3. Seeded regional pools merged other profiles merely because they shared a
   staple. Japanese recipes could inherit curry-only turmeric from Japanese curry.
   Pools now contain the selected authored profile plus explicit curated extras;
   they no longer merge all profiles sharing rice/bread. Unseeded profiles remain
   on their authored ingredients. Signature heat, exclusions and variation remain.

4. Deferred side timing told even a raw garden salad to stay hot. The schedule
   now follows that side's own preparation and serving instructions.

5. Nearby shops showed a premature zero before an area/search existed. Phone
   labels now distinguish choosing an area, finding shops and completed results.
   The first price action explicitly says Choose area to compare. Price lookups
   remain deliberate; unavailable prices and branch stock remain unconfirmed.

6. Live retailer checking exposed a rice-form mismatch: Woolworths product
   528193 has a generic Family Size Jasmine Rice title, but its ingredient and
   category metadata identify cooked rice. That evidence now travels through
   catalogue matching and blocks both automatic and manual dry-rice quantity
   costing for that product. Pack weight alone is not used to guess the form.
   Actual dry 450 g packs remain eligible. Published pack prices stay visible.

Older saved variations are preserved. Those with the former protein/format
additions receive a refresh prompt when opened. Create generates a new variation
with the tighter rules rather than silently rewriting saved cooking content.

Validation

- 339 final regression tests passed, zero failures (41 suites). This rerun excludes
  the expensive unchanged meals.test.ts Cartesian baseline; the new coherence
  matrix explicitly covers 1,344 seeded complete dishes, every compatible main/
  method, all meal regions and every classic/profile pairing.
- Regional pool tests include 768 Japanese seed/heat cases and 768 other-family
  cases; Greek-to-Japanese isolation, deterministic seeds, scaling, exclusions,

- TypeScript passed. Touched-file lint returned zero errors and five existing

  docs/audit-evidence-2026-10-27.

Interpretation of the supplied audit

The regional carry-over appearance and loose finishes were actionable findings.
Fresh generation itself uses the new regional inputs rather than mutating the
previous recipe. Japanese halloumi can intentionally remain halloumi when only
the flavour changes; the user's selected main and side are not silently replaced.

Local Shops is not intrinsically data-empty: published online guide prices can
be fetched after choosing an area and Compare prices. Retailer coverage varies;
both Woolworths and Coles returned real priced products during this follow-up.
local shelf stock is not verified. Empty Trends reflects absent real cook reports,
not failed navigation. No community reports or prices were fabricated.

These checks establish structural consistency and functional behavior. They do
not establish that a recipe was cooked, tasted or is a traditional regional dish.
Recipes retain Template-generated · not kitchen-tested labelling.
