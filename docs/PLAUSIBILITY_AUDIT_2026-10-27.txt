FUSION TABLE - WEBSITE AND ANDROID PLAUSIBILITY AUDIT
27 October 2026

Scope and standard

This pass reviewed actual generated meals and the rules composing them: identity,
ingredient form and purpose, portions, filling capacity, grain liquids, regional
flavour, texture, cooking sequence, equipment and realistic time. The earlier
large matrix checked structure; it did not prove that food combinations made
culinary sense. This pass adds focused tests for observed semantic failures.
The user's Aussie-Thai parma/steak-wrap benchmark remains the reference for a
recognisable main, purposeful additions and a practical method.

Concrete problems corrected

1. Chosen cuts: liver inherited beef's roughly two-hour simmer. It now has a short
   final cook of evenly sliced pieces to 75 C, without a preceding long sear.
   Bone-in purchased weights allow for bones; four-serving chicken marylands now
   use 1.5 kg rather than 600 g. Whole racks become individual rib cutlets for
   quick direct heat. Bone-in shanks/ribs/hocks cannot enter a home-mincer patty
   method. Paneer schnitzel/grilling uses broad slabs, not cubes.
2. Small-batch cooking: thin beef strips previously received 7-10 minutes PER
   batch. The instruction now distinguishes a short batch from the total
   household batch time, divides oil, limits crowding, and preserves thermometer
   endpoints. Fragile fish is turned gently. Long-cook ranges avoid false
   minute-level precision. Conflicting oven temperatures now have an explicit
   one-oven sequence with prompt side cooling/refrigeration when needed and a
   thorough reheat during the main's rest; the estimate budgets that sequence.
3. Classic plate feasibility: stuffed shells no longer receive extra buns.
   Filling is about 30 g per open jumbo shell, pasta is packet-cooked, and the
   filled shells cook moist and covered before a brief uncovered finish. Long
   cannelloni tubes are not interchangeable aliases. Burnt ends receive their
   delicate miso/herb/citrus finish after the long pan stage. Pie/BBQ estimates
   include required serial cooking and rests. Seeded prep is ready before a
   quick main finishes, preserving crisp coatings and hot pasta.
4. Sides and regional forms: whole sorghum has a 50-60-minute window. Freekeh
   and farro specify cracked/pearled forms and longer-substitute directions.
   Soft-grain liquid starting points scale; risotto reserves finishing liquid.
   Garlic/ginger/onion powders and chilli flakes no longer receive fresh-piece
   preparation. Counted whole cloves/cinnamon are removable infusions. Implicit
   water scales for already-scaled callers, and delicate-only sauces have an
   actual moist base. No unsupported blanket salt/heat reductions were made.
5. Android composition: sweet fruit/yoghurt/cereal breakfasts reject savoury
   chilli remixes with a clear explanation. Re-fusing an existing fusion is
   blocked so sauces do not stack. Warm meals keep a compatible warm finish;
   genuine cold meals receive ready-mix dressings, avoiding an unbudgeted
   cook-then-fridge wait. Added acid/hot-condiment/strong-condiment lanes are
   bounded. The household heat setting now affects newly added chilli while
   preserving the saved main, and the visible copy matches that contract.
   Studio plates include a measured vegetable portion. Cooked-rice dinner and
   lunch quantities are explicit and appropriate to the four-serving yield.
   Seven native sides have meaningful named flavours/dressings; barley has
   BARLEY/GLUTEN tags rather than a false wheat tag.

Limits

The result is a reviewed template catalogue with explicit guards, not proof of
universal culinary authenticity, taste, nutrition or successful cooking. Actual
packet contents, equipment capacity, cross-contact and kitchen timings require
real checks. Saved user methods/snapshots are retained; generating a fresh meal
uses current rules. Templates remain clearly labelled not kitchen-tested.
