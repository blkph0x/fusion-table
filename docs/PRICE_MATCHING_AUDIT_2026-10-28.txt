Store choices and unknown prices

Cheapest per item compares eligible whole-pack quantity costs across sellers.
A chain-only choice keeps the selected supermarket; an unavailable line remains
unknown rather than being filled by another chain or a planning allowance.
When local specialists are enabled, fresh meat and produce follow the selected
located butcher/fruit shop and the remaining items follow the supermarket rule.
An unpriced specialist stays assigned on the list with an unknown price.
Unknown lines do not count as zero or as part of a complete basket total.

Shop IDs and distances are revalidated against the current area and radius.
An unavailable explicit shop is not silently replaced. Area-specific choices,
reloads, CSV export, radius changes and mobile layout have browser-fixture proof.
Online price guides remain separate from verified local shelf stock or a single
confirmed retailer basket. Recipe ingredient planning values are labelled AUD
budget assumptions and do not replace unknown prices in the chosen-shop total.

The original shop-choice public UI fixture was recorded on release
20261028-072139/version0.4.0. A separate public-build fixture first asserted the
exact 082209/version0.4.1 deployment identity, then injected the four unsafe
eligible/chosen rows. All four stayed unknown and out of seller coverage and
subtotals; a valid rice control alone remained priced. Re-entering the shopping
view reused that same unsafe cache without another price request and remained
safe. Equivalent positive product/form controls priced successfully. All APIs
and images were intercepted in those UI checks: they are not supplier evidence.

The local follow-up additionally keeps hot-roast/rotisserie/cooked foods,
Peking/confit/char-siu/tandoori/heat-and-serve foods, recipe bases and finished
foods such as pesto, ginger beer, garlic bread and carrot cake under the selected
supermarket instead of routing them to fresh specialists. Raw Pekin duck breed,
braising/stewing cuts, raw roasting joints, BBQ blade steak and roasting potatoes
remain supported. Twenty-five focused strategy tests pass. This correction
requires the next deployment before it can be claimed as public behaviour.

Recipe plausibility and discovery

The strongest current recipe audit passed 94,100 distinct cases: 100 rotating
inputs for each of 907 cooking rows plus 100 for each of 34 classic structures.
The 907 rows comprise 266 default main/method rows and 641 compatible chosen-cut
rows. Each row covers all 76 region values and all 90 side values, with varying
servings, heat, seed and compatible preferences. It does not cover all 6,840
region/side pairs for every row. Another 907 one-versus-twelve serving pairs and
fifty exact share rebuilds passed. Structural checks do not establish taste,
successful kitchen execution, actual doneness or packaged-food contents.

Discover expanded from 120 to 1,705 distinct main/method/region cores, a 14.2-fold
increase. Side-only variants do not inflate that number. The pool represents
30 mains, 13 methods, 76 regions and 90 sides. Fifty audited shuffles generated
300 exact card/share recipes, seeing 293 distinct cores and no repeats inside
the bounded 120-core recent-history window. Strict filters are not relaxed to
fill a card batch. The native app improves selection from its existing offline
catalogue; it does not claim the website's 1,705-core pool was copied into Android.
