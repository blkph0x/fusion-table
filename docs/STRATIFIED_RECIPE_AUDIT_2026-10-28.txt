Concrete corrections found in manual review and regression work:
- Smoked meat copied rest instructions from endpoint prose into cooking, stall and rest stages, with conflicting durations for ribs and turkey. Cooking instructions now retain endpoint/tenderness guidance while resting is one dedicated stage using its authored duration. The cook estimate includes that rest once.
- A 180-minute smoke was split into 180-minute bark plus a minimum 60-minute finishing stage, despite a 180-minute total budget. Such cooks now partition 120 + 60 minutes; longer cooks retain 180 + remaining minutes.
- The wider discovery share tests exposed legacy side IDs containing -veg- being mistaken for a vegetarian constraint. The share parser now reads flags only after the diet/heat/servings boundary. Fifty audit witnesses independently rebuild exact cooking content.

Further review: all 1,705 expanded discovery cores generated and passed structure/portion/timing checks with no strong regional seasoning leaks. Five old repetitive main-and-side component combinations were passed to the discovery owner and normalized. Chosen cuts should be shown explicitly in the UI because a generic title such as roast turkey does not explain that turkey mince is formed into patties.

The large 50-recipe witness corpus stays local under work/recipe-50-audit/live-50.json. It includes all 30 mains, 13 methods, 50 regions and 50 sides, including whole-bird, liver, brisket, shank and mince examples.

Boundaries: these checks inspect recorded content, arithmetic, identity and instructions. They do not establish taste, actual successful cooking, actual doneness, packet contents, allergen cross-contact or clinical dietary suitability. Explicit cross-regional sides are allowed fusion inputs; they are separated from accidental main-seasoning leakage. Recipe time and texture remain estimates, with packet guidance, thermometer endpoints and tenderness checks prevailing.
