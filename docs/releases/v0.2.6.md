# Fusion Table v0.2.6 — a new plate each time you ask

Create my fusion uses a new seed on every click. A chicken and Thai schnitzel is no longer the same template twice. The solver can change the finish: a cooked schnitzel can be sliced into a cold stir-fry with fish, oyster sauce, sriracha, bird's eye chillies and lime, or it can come back as a hot stir-fry, a chopped salad, a lettuce wrap, or a broth. Plan my week uses the seed to order the dinners, so the week is not an alphabetical list, and each dinner carries its own seeded finish.

The chooser is a small CDCL solver: unit propagation, a first unique implication point, and a backjump, in the shape of the local P versus NP research solver. The research solver always branches false and therefore has one model. This one branches in an order taken from the seed. The assignment is checked against the clauses. Diet, allergen and hard dislikes are clauses. They are not relaxed.

A cold plate is cooked, then chilled. It is not a raw dish. Added fish is cooked to at least 63°C. Added prawns are cooked until pink and opaque. Poultry keeps the endpoint already in the template. Nothing here is kitchen-tested, allergy-safe by packet, or a result about P versus NP.

The phone app composes the same kind of plate when a cuisine is tapped. The week on the phone is still a shuffle of the templates already stored there.

This note describes the source. It is not itself a deployment.
