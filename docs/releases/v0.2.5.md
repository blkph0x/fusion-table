# Fusion Table v0.2.5 — catalogue prices and meat cuts

The shopping list can check the Woolworths online catalogue. A shown price came back from that catalogue. It is not the shelf at a local Woolworths, and it is not a butcher or fruit-shop board.

Coles blocks an automatic check. ALDI, butchers and fruit shops have no public price feed. A price you type in is labelled as the one you recorded. A missing price stays blank and is never $0.

Chicken, beef, lamb and pork can take a chosen cut, including breast or thigh, ordinary beef, Angus and wagyu. Leaving the cut on “match the cooking method” keeps the previous recipe. Check the packet. A breed name is not a marble grade.

Templates stay “Template-generated · not kitchen-tested”. This note is not a deployment. The live site stays on the build already running until that build is replaced.
