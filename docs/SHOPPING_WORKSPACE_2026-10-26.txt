FUSION TABLE — LOCAL SHOPPING WORKSPACE
26 October 2026

The Local shops view is now one shopping workspace. It uses the selected suburb
and radius, a recipe/week/saved/custom ingredient list, nearby food-shop listings,
published catalogue offers and shop-specific recorded prices.

How to use it

1. Open Local shops, or Make shopping list on a recipe/week.
2. Check the area at the top. Nearby shops load automatically; changing the radius
   starts a new search after a short pause.
3. Choose the recipe, week or saved list, or add your own ingredients and amounts.
4. Choose Compare prices. Results appear in batches for the complete list.
5. Review suggested products, choose another brand/pack, or exclude a line. The
   estimate counts the packs needed to buy the requested amount, rather than
   pretending one pack always covers a recipe.
6. Select a nearby shop to see its details directly below that card. Use the
   retailer product links, directions, phone or website. Record an observed price
   with its pack size to add a local comparison.

Phones have Prices / Nearby shops controls so a long weekly list does not bury
store search. The page fits a 320-pixel viewport. Record forms load their shop
options only when opened, avoiding thousands of hidden controls on weekly lists.

Price coverage and evidence

- Woolworths public search JSON and Coles public search-page product data are
  read without accounts, cookies, CAPTCHA workarounds or paid API calls.
- Their online guide prices are not assigned to a nearby branch as confirmed
  stock or shelf prices. Woolworths' default fulfilment-store identifier is not
  treated as the user's local store.
- A Coles search returned real product/pack prices during testing; a later search
  returned an interruption page. That case is handled as unavailable, retains
  other results, provides a direct retailer link and triggers a short backoff.
  Retailer failure never becomes a free item or a fabricated quote.
- Both chain cards remain visible after comparison, including unknown cost and
  coverage when a source cannot be read. Local recorded prices are separate.
- Local records are tied to the selected area and an exact shop identifier when
  one is available. Same-name branches are not silently merged. Generic GPS
  locations require a suburb/postcode before local records can be used or saved.
- Catalogue prices cover Australia. Known overseas locations keep store browsing
  and links, while Australian prices/records are not borrowed for their postcode.

Calculation safeguards

- Exact metric conversions, count packs, multipacks and stated per-unit rates
  are supported. Approximate/drained/cooked conversions and weight-versus-volume
  assumptions remain unknown. Old recorded prices without pack sizes stay visible
  without being treated as a complete recipe quantity.
- Safe plain-rice and firm-tofu brand ties can produce suggested pack estimates;
  requested words and food form must match. Prepared rice/flavoured tofu and
  unknown dietary claims cannot become automatic substitutes. Meat/fish cut ties
  remain explicit review choices.
- Freshness, expiry, unknown stock, quantities and partial basket coverage are
  shown. A smaller subtotal for fewer ingredients is not called a cheaper full
  basket. User choices and exclusions survive a price refresh in the same context.
- Custom lists and bought ticks persist on this device. Shopping ticks do not
  consume pantry stock or rewrite the underlying recipe.

Validation

318 final regression tests passed with no failures. TypeScript passed; lint returned
zero errors and the eight existing image/unused-variable advisories. Browser
checks, production-build results and deployment identity are recorded under
docs/audit-evidence-2026-10-26 and in the final shopping deployment record.

Real price examples are dated observations, not fixed application prices. One
750 g firm-tofu + 450 g jasmine-rice check suggested a full-pack subtotal of
A$8.15; the source prices can change. Synthetic local price-entry checks used an
isolated local development ledger and are excluded from deployment data.
