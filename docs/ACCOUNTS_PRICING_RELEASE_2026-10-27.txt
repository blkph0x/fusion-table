Delivered behaviour

Every authored ingredient now has a displayed AUD value in What you will need.
Compatible current retailer guides are used where available; the rest use
explicitly labelled, versioned planning allowances. Per-ingredient values cover
the quantity put into the recipe, including pantry food. The automatic shopping
budget separately counts the packs or weighed amounts still to buy, with
leftovers. It never promotes an allowance into a published retailer quote.

All ten Discover meals (weekly pick, six shuffled suggestions, three classic
remixes) have a recipe total, per-serving cost and actual serving count. Values
render immediately before external lookups. Visitors without a saved location
are prompted to choose a suburb/postcode, use precise location explicitly, or
continue with labelled Australian planning estimates. No automatic precise
location request is made. Non-Australian areas retain AUD allowances, not a
claim of prices in their local currency.

Pantry checkboxes immediately update the automatic shopping shortfall and the
saved kitchen. They declare ownership without consuming stock. Reversing a new
tick preserves known fractional quantities; repeated/duplicate uses do not
inflate stock, and undoing a cook cannot overwrite newer pantry changes.
The ingredient's food-used value remains visible when it is already owned.
An actual browser check changed a 13-line A$58.30 shopping budget to 12 lines
and A$45.30 after declaring the A$13 halloumi already owned.

Optional accounts use the host's installed MariaDB software in an isolated,
loopback-only instance on port 3307. The existing MariaDB on 3306 is unchanged.
Guests keep browser data. Account kitchens are private SQL snapshots, restored
only after confirmed authentication and an explicit choice to import the guest
kitchen or use the account copy. Signed-in content does not overwrite the guest
browser kitchen. Household settings, area, pantry, cookbook, shopping and week
plan sync automatically with account-ID and version checks for competing tabs.
Signing out restores the guest kitchen. Blocked browser storage has a tab-local
fallback for account operation.

Passwords use independently salted, bounded asynchronous scrypt. SQL stores
hashed opaque session tokens, and HTTPS cookies use Secure, HttpOnly and
SameSite=Lax with the /fusion/ path. Ordinary sessions have a 12-hour server
limit; remembered sessions have 30-day absolute and 7-day inactivity limits.
Password changes and sign-out on all devices revoke old sessions. SQL-backed
attempt limits, strict same-origin mutation checks, bounded JSON and prepared
queries are enabled. Email ownership verification and email password recovery
are not configured; this is stated on signup. No such feature is claimed.

Price gathering and defects fixed

Provider caches now share duplicate work across meals, retain bounded 512-query
results, cancel abandoned work, back off after blocked responses and cap streamed
responses. Fixes include fresh onions matching onion gravy, reversed produce
wording, missed one-litre bottle conversions, preparation suffixes polluting
searches, and safe common-food brand matches. Legacy seeded produce gets a
fresh-produce allowance even if its old category was specialty. Preserved foods,
wrong food forms, ambiguous weighed prices and nonfood products remain excluded.

Coles did not supply readable results during these checks; its limitation is
shown and is not bypassed. On the production connection, 55 representative
ingredient lines from 24 generated meals all had planning values; eight also
had compatible observed store guides. This is complete budgeting coverage, not
complete live local pricing, stock availability, or a guaranteed one-shop basket.
Unsupported arbitrary custom units still need a manual quantity rather than a
guessed conversion. Profile sizes, densities and yields are disclosed assumptions.
