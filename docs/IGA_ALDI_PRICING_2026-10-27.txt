IGA and ALDI public-price verification — 27 October 2026

Scope
These adapters read selling prices actually published by the Australian retailer.
They do not infer stock, national consistency, the visitor's local store, or a price
from a missing result. A renderer, when configured, is used for every page check.
It must use ordinary browser behavior. Refusals cause bounded failure/backoff,
not CAPTCHA solving, stealth, proxy rotation, or repeated challenge retries.

ALDI

Observed category:
https://www.aldi.com.au/products/meat-seafood/poultry/k/1111111146
Example product:
https://www.aldi.com.au/product/imperial-grain-jasmine-rice-1kg-000000000000262101

The public page renders product-tile-{id} cards. Name, brand, selling size and
comparison unit price have separate product-tile__* data-test attributes.
The observed base-price__regular span contains the selling price. Comparison
unit prices are never substituted for selling prices. Unrecognized discount
layouts, membership/coupon prices and announced 'On Sale' cards are omitted.
This deliberately errs toward missing an offer instead of quoting a future one.

Observed examples, for validation rather than constants in application code:
- Jasmine Rice 1kg: $2.99, comparison $0.30 per 100g.
- Chicken Breast Fillets Bulk: approximately 1.38kg at $15.17; $10.99/kg comparison.
- Chicken Breast Fillets: approximately 0.6kg at $7.19; $11.99/kg comparison.

The ordinary browser automatically showed '2745, Glenmore Park'. This was not a
visitor-selected store and is not used as the visitor's location. Results are
labelled an online guide, with unknown stock, never a local shelf or stock check.
Approximate weighted packages remain approximate; exact shopping calculations
must refuse them. Fixed packs and count packs retain their payable pack sizes.

IGA
Observed normal-browser search route:
https://www.igashop.com.au/search?q=jasmine+rice
Example product:
https://www.igashop.com.au/product/chicken-breast-fillets-80080001

Selecting a store through IGA's visible postcode/store chooser changes browser
session context without changing the product/search URL. A public sample area
Newtown NSW 2042 was used for verification; no precise user location was shared.
The same chicken page initially displayed Ashcroft's IGA Erskine Park at $6.50
average 500g / $13/kg. After explicitly selecting Lloyds IGA Newtown it displayed
$7.50 average 500g / $15/kg. Thus a generic IGA result cannot be called national
or assigned to the nearest IGA. The page also initially said no store selected.

The adapter requires an explicit server-verified IgaStoreContext, then checks the
rendered header-store-select strong text exactly against its branch name. Missing
or different store selection returns needs-store and no prices. The renderer
must already hold the genuinely selected branch context. Without verified branch
selection the browser gateway must leave IGA in needs-store.

Normal visible store selection flow: header-store-select-button, then optional
'Search for another store', 'Shop in-store' tab, #search-location postcode, exact
suburb role=option, then the matching branch card's Select Store button.
Branch cards expose data-test-id=store-selection-list-item and a public
data-store-id attribute (Lloyds Newtown 8863, Enmore 6033, Stanmore 6743).
Each includes h3 branch name, street/suburb/postcode, and its own Select Store
button. Both branch name and address should agree with server-verified nearby
shop details; never choose the first branch or infer a branch from the postcode.
After selection wait for the rendered header and cards to update before reading.

Rendered IGA cards use data-product-card=true. Their labelled product anchor has
separate name and pack spans; the bold leading-none span is the selling price.
Comparison prices and 'was' badges are not payable prices. __NEXT_DATA__ is not
used: client-side branch selection can leave stale default-store data there.
All returned stock flags remain unknown unless the card explicitly says out of
stock/unavailable. 'Add To List' does not establish availability.

Example selected-store rendered observations:
- SunRice Premium Thai Jasmine Rice 1kg: $8.10.
- SunRice Jasmine Fragrant Rice 5kg: current $16.95, was $27.00, comparison $3.39/kg.
- Chicken Breast Fillets 500g: $7.50 average, final cost based on weight.

Operational bounds
Each adapter caps HTML at 3MB, limits result cards and products, uses bounded
concurrency/queues, deduplicates simultaneous reads, caches for ten minutes, and
backs off unreadable/refused pages for one minute. Renderer caches are isolated
by renderer identity; IGA cache keys additionally include branch ID/name/area.
Redirects and product links remain on the official secure retailer origin.
No real cookies or account credentials are stored in this repository.

Coles browser compatibility was also extended using the currently rendered
section[data-testid=product-tile] cards, product-pricing span and separately
labelled pack size. The current card price wins over stale __NEXT_DATA__ prices.
Observed normal-browser six-card sample retained $15.40 / approx.1.4kg chicken
breast, $9.00 / 500g diced chicken (ignoring its separate 2-for-$13.50 offer), and
$8.00 / 300g smoked chicken (ignoring old/comparison prices). Default retailer
delivery context 'Melbourne 3000' is labelled as retailer context, not the visitor.
All adapters preserve gateway challenge/configuration status across backoff.

The shared matcher now rejects raw meat/seafood requests against cooked, smoked,
breaded, marinated, cured or deli products unless that preparation is explicitly
requested. Matching-name proof for unknown-stock guides has the same gate.
Frenched cuts, raw diced meat, roasting cuts and skin/bone preparation stay valid.

Focused checks
tests/aldi-catalogue.test.ts and tests/iga-catalogue.test.ts cover selling versus
comparison/previous prices, future/member offers, fixed/count/approximate packs,
stock uncertainty, branch leakage, renderer preference, cache context isolation,
response limits, foreign redirects, product matching and provider backoff.
